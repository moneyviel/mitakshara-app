Download Mitakshara

The private, offline-first workspace for advocates. You need the licence key sent to your email after purchase. One key works on one computer; reinstalling on the same computer uses the same key.

WindowsWindows 10 and 11 Mac (Apple chip)M1, M2, M3, M4 Mac (Intel)Older Macs
Windows: if a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info and then Run anyway.
Mac: open the downloaded zip and drag Mitakshara into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open. If the Mac still refuses, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip; "Processor: Intel" means Intel.
After installing, Mitakshara updates itself from the Settings page.
